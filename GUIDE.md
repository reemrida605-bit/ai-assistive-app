# How to Use the AI Visual Assistant

A voice-first app that uses your phone camera and AI to describe what is around you — in Arabic or English.

---

## First Time Setup

1. Open the app in **Chrome on Android**
2. Tap **Install** to add it to your home screen
3. Say **"عربي"** to use Arabic, or say **"English"** to use English
4. Allow camera and microphone when asked

---

## How It Works

1. **Point** your phone camera at what you want described
2. **Say a command** — the app is always listening, no tap needed
3. **Listen** — the AI speaks the result aloud in your language
4. After the result, say **"كرر" / "repeat"** to hear it again

---

## What to Say

### From the home screen

| You say | What happens |
|---|---|
| **ماذا أمامي** / **what is in front of me** | Describes everything visible |
| **اقرأ النص** / **read text** | Reads any sign, label or document |
| **صف المكان** / **describe scene** | Describes the full place you are in |
| **ساعدني في التنقل** / **help me navigate** | Warns about steps, hazards, obstacles |
| **يمين** / **right** | Describes what is on your right side |
| **يسار** / **left** | Describes what is on your left side |

### After you get a result

| You say | What happens |
|---|---|
| **كرر** / **repeat** | Reads the result again |
| **تحليل مرة أخرى** / tap button | Takes a new photo and analyses it |
| **الرئيسية** / **home** | Goes back to home screen |
| **رجوع** / **back** | Goes to previous screen |
| **اسكت** / **stop** | Stops speaking immediately |

---

## Tips

- Hold the phone at chest height pointing forward for best results
- Speak naturally — "describe scene" and "describe the scene" both work
- In noisy places, tap the big circle button on screen instead of speaking
- The app needs internet to analyse images
- If voice stops responding, say **"اسكت" / "stop"** and try again
